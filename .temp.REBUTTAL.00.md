# 4. Tighten the PostgreSQL HA contract

Retain two independently owned three-member Patroni groups and three shared DCS voters:

**Vector domain:** authoritative `lme_vectors` and `litellm`, initially written on graph.

Preserve the strict synchronous acceptance profile: Patroni synchronous mode, strict mode, one required synchronous standby, and application transactions using synchronous commit. Patroni distinguishes ordinary synchronous mode from strict mode and documents circumstances where transaction cancellation or explicit client overrides weaken the intended guarantees. Therefore, test acknowledged application commits rather than inferring durability from configuration alone. ([Patroni][6])

### Logical-slot continuity must be an acceptance dependency

Verify the PostgreSQL 17 failover-slot prerequisites: failover-enabled logical slots, standby slot synchronization, physical slots, `hot_standby_feedback`, and appropriate connection configuration. Before accepting a failover candidate, verify the complete required slot inventory—not just one representative slot—including persistence and absence of invalidation. ([PostgreSQL][7])

Handle `synchronized_standby_slots` deliberately. Logical delivery waits for the listed physical slots, so a missing or invalid member can block it. Reconcile dead members only through a procedure that also preserves fencing and promotion eligibility; deleting a slot merely to make lag disappear is not recovery. ([postgresql.org][8])

Exercise publisher failover, subscriber failover, and failure of a guest containing members of **both** domains. Verify reconnection, continued logical progress, denied application writes to imported tables, and correct vector queries.

### Fencing must account for co-location

Do not give two independent Patroni groups an unexamined shared watchdog arrangement. A watchdog can reset the whole guest, affecting both database members and its DCS voter. Establish one coordinated fencing design and test its full failure scope. Patroni explicitly identifies stalled processes and paused VMs as cases requiring protection beyond ordinary graceful demotion. ([Patroni][9])

No fencing path may reset the physical silmaril host or affect its Windows VM.

### Migrations and restore remain initial requirements

Keep migration identities separate from runtime identities. Retain publication-safe relations during the `docs_chunks` reset; coordinate HertzBeat and LiteLLM schema changes across authoritative and imported copies. PostgreSQL logical replication does not propagate DDL or sequence state. ([PostgreSQL][10])

Backups, archived WAL, slot-retention monitoring, and isolated restore must pass before declaring the database stage complete. Validate `shared-ha` separately; do not silently substitute it when `dual-owned-ha` fails.

### A local spool is not automatically host-loss protection

Retain the 4 GiB log spool, 2 GiB free-disk reserve, 24-hour retention after complete verification, and 256 MiB persistent metric queues per destination.

But distinguish three claims:

**Durably accepted** means the record has reached the promised durability boundary. **Transport acknowledged** means a downstream transport accepted it. **Verified delivered** means the intended sink returned the expected content.

If acknowledged ingress records are included in the single-host-loss guarantee, acceptance requires durability outside that host’s failure domain. A node-local spool alone cannot establish that guarantee. Account for any replicated journal/outbox in the database and resource budgets without introducing Kafka.

At quota exhaustion, stop acknowledging records that cannot be durably retained. Apply backpressure where supported and expose an explicit unavailable/loss condition where a source cannot retry. Never purge unverified records to keep the dashboard green.

The five-minute recovery requirement also needs an admitted workload envelope. Measure backlog, concurrent incoming rate, replay throughput, indexing delay, and verification time. An unbounded outage backlog cannot be promised to drain within a fixed interval.

Finally, **database HA is not whole-application HA**. Measure the 90-second reconnection target for surviving application instances reconnecting to the safe writer. A sole application instance located on the failed guest remains unavailable until that application is restored.
